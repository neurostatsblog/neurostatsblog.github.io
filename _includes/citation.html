{%- comment -%} Renders a BibTeX citation block for a post. When the post has an `arxiv:` id in its front matter, that preprint is the thing to cite: the block emits an @article with eprint/archivePrefix and arXiv's registered DOI (10.48550/arXiv.), which is what Scholar and reference managers de-duplicate against. Posts with no preprint fall back to an @misc pointing at the post URL. Optional front matter: arxiv the bare id, e.g. 2607.12345 arxiv_class primary category, e.g. q-bio.NC arxiv_title preprint title, when it differs from the post title (arXiv has one title field; posts split title/subtitle) bibtex_key overrides the generated _ key bibtex_author author string in BibTeX form ("Last, First" / "A and B"). Defaults to the display name, which BibTeX parses correctly but less robustly than surname-first. Note: only the first author is emitted. Fine for single-author posts; a co-authored one needs `bibtex_author` set by hand. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign first_author = page.authors[0].name | default: page.author | default: site.author.name -%} {%- assign cite_author = page.bibtex_author | default: first_author -%} {%- assign last_name = first_author | split: " " | last | downcase -%} {%- assign year = page.date | date: "%Y" -%} {%- assign slug_first = page.slug | split: "-" | first -%} {%- assign auto_key = last_name | append: year | append: "_" | append: slug_first -%} {%- assign cite_key = page.bibtex_key | default: auto_key -%} {%- assign post_url = page.url | absolute_url -%} {%- assign cite_title = page.arxiv_title | default: page.title -%} {%- assign lb = "{" -%} {%- assign rb = "}" -%}
